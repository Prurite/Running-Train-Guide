# Preface

> Content mainly made by `@3N[CN/JP/EN]`, converted to website & edited by `@prurite`.

This is just the beta edition of the manual. Therefore, many things are subject to
change during the updating process.

Currently, it contains 5 chapters.

In Chapter 0, there is a small article Railway 101 written by me, previously
posted on The Ultimate Guide for SimRail. This serves as an introductory discussion
on some basic knowledge of railways, as some non-railfans also have bought the
game based on the news from players in other servers.

In Chapter 1, I give a brief introduction to Japanese railways. This includes
signals and signs you need to know, together with some logics on operations and
signal aspects.

In Chapter 2, some information on trains in this game is given. Here I list some
hints for driving these types based on my own driving experience. Besides, prototypes
of all EMU/DMUs are listed. Hopping you can also learn something about types you
are going to drive in this chapter.

Chapter 3 is quite short as it only deals with 2 devices you need to look at. This
chapter may be extended to include more things, but since you cannot click on
devices to control, it is still just a plan.

Chapter 4 is for those who want to seek authentic Japanese driving styles. I listed
a small introduction to Japanese pronunciation, and talk about some basic yubisashi
while dividing corresponded Romaji into syllables, so that you can read them out. I
excluded phonological rules for Japanese to avoid being too academical, if you really
want to learn Japanese language, it is better to check some real textbooks than this.

For many years, Japanese train simulators are dominated by video tapes and all
kinds of small products. Hope this game would eventually bring some revolutionary
air into the industry, and open a window for people to get in touch with Japanese
railways.
